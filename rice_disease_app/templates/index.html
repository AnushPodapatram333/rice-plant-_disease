Instrument note — validation accuracy peaked at 97.4% on epoch 25, but EarlyStopping was watching validation loss, not accuracy. Its lowest point came at epoch 21 (loss 0.076, 97.2% accuracy); five epochs then passed with no further improvement, so training stopped at epoch 26/30 and the restored weights are epoch 21's — the most generalizable point, not simply the highest-scoring one.
